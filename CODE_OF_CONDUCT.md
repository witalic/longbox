# Code of conduct

longbox is made by one person and whoever joins in. Everyone taking part — in issues, pull
requests, discussions and reviews — is expected to:

- be kind and assume good faith, especially with people new to the project or to English;
- criticise ideas and code, never the person behind them;
- keep to the subject, and take a disagreement that has stopped being useful elsewhere.

Harassment, insults, slurs, sexual remarks and attention, personal attacks, and publishing
anyone's private information are not tolerated, in any form.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close threads, and block anyone who does not keep to
this. If something here makes you uncomfortable, use GitHub's **Report content** on the comment,
or report it privately to the maintainer through the repository's **Security** tab → **Report a
vulnerability** and say it is about conduct.
