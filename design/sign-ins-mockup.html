Where sign-ins live

Each vault says where the embedded browser keeps its sign-ins and site data: in the vault (they travel with it) or on each computer (the vault holds none). The launch window asks once — for every existing vault, and for a new one right after it is made — with two answers of equal weight and nothing preselected. Settings → Browser changes it either way; the profile moves on the next start. Design note: design/sign-ins.md.
