Contents views

The entries of a title, seen four ways — chosen in the Contents head and remembered per type (the title's shelf). The column moves to the right, where the reader and the player keep their list: what is open is the big surface on the left, what comes next runs down beside it. List is today's rows. Tiles keeps the column as wide as it is and stacks a card per entry down it. Wall gives the cards the whole width; a card opens the reader or the player directly. Each card is a 16:9 preview with the row's facts under it. Pages is for image sets: every page of every entry as one wall, under the entry it belongs to — and the entries under their section and language.

The card

  • An episode wears its poster — the frame the vault already cuts for it.
  • A page entry whose first page is wider than tall (a spread, an artbook) wears that page whole, like a poster.
  • Otherwise three of its pages side by side — picked at random, but the same three every time: the pick is seeded by the entry and its version, so it only changes when its pages do.
  • Under it, the row's facts: number and label; read mark, language · group, CC, and pages or duration.

What it does

  • Tiles: a click opens the entry in the pane, exactly as a row does now.
  • Wall: no pane — a click opens the reader at the entry, or plays the episode in the player.
  • A card per entry: each translation is its own card; LANG and GROUP narrow them as they narrow the rows.
  • Sections head their cards across the whole row. An entry with no file is a dashed card that opens nothing.
  • A title in more than one language shows each as a block of its own, its sections inside — on Tiles, the Wall and Pages alike.

Pages

  • Every page of every entry, a wall of thumbnails; a page opens the reader right there.
  • Each entry heads its own pages — number, label, read mark, language · group, page count — so where one set ends and the next begins is never a guess.
  • Entries sit under their section, and on a title in more than one language each language is a block of its own, as on the Wall.
  • Offered where there are pages to show: a title of episodes only does not list it. An entry with no file is a dashed line under its heading.
  • Columns 4–12, as Tiles 1–2 and Wall 3–8. Thumbnails load as they scroll into view.

Remembered

  • The view and the columns (Tiles 1–2, Wall 3–8) belong to the type — every manga alike, every collection alike — kept with the window's settings in the vault, so they travel with the library.
  • A type never chosen for: a title of episodes opens on the Wall, one of page entries on the List.
  • Editing contents (✎) shows the list — dragging and the row actions live there — and Done returns to the view chosen.
  • A title with nothing to open yet (no entry has a file) has no pane: its list starts at the left, as it does today.
