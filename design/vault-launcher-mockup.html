Vault launcher

What the app shows before it opens a vault (design/vault-home.md). None of it appears when the last vault answers within about a second — the app opens straight into it, as today. It is a small window of its own, drawn before any sidecar exists, and its theme follows the system: the theme the human chose lives in the vault it has not opened yet.

A second copy of the app on the SAME computer is not an in use case: it hands its window over to the one already running.

The browser's tabs are closed for now; they reopen with the app.

The changes to Conqueror Mona you had not saved cannot be saved until the vault is back — and are lost when the app restarts.

Reconnecting — longbox restarts into LongBox as soon as it answers, and reopens your tabs.

Lost is a fact, not a guess. The sidecar reads vault.json every 5 s with a 5 s deadline, and at once after any network error: missing, unreadable, another vault's id or no answer means lost. From then every request is refused at once (nothing waits on the drive), background work stops, and no directory is made under a root that does not hold its vault.json — so an emptied mount point or another share under the same letter is never written to. Two good answers in a row and the app restarts into the vault: fresh index, fresh browser profile, the tabs it had. The window lists only what actually stopped; the unsaved-edit line appears only when there is one.
