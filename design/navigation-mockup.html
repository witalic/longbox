Places, tabs and the way back

Today only a title can be come back to. Here everything the app shows is a place, any place lives in a tab, and a tab remembers how it got there and what you did in it — a value's page, the library under its filters, Metadata half-way through an operation — until you close it. Concept: design/navigation.md.

5 · DECISIONS

One decided, two with a proposal

D1 · THE STRIP · DECIDED
The app and the web keep separate strips

The owner's decision. The app strip gains pages, library tabs and sections; the web strip stays as it is.

D2 · NAMING
The rail's Browse becomes Web

Proposed. Browse by then means one thing — the library grouped by a field. The other way: keep Browse.

D3 · A VALUE'S CLICK
A value opens its page, everywhere

Proposed. Filtering moves to Show in library on the page and the hover card. The other way: a title's chips keep filtering the library as today.
