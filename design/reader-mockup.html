Reader v2 — a hideable sidebar owns navigation and modes

Reading happens INSIDE the title's tab (← back / Esc returns to the title page). All navigation and display controls live in a right sidebar that hides with one click (S) — hidden, the reader is just the page and two click-zones. Vault-only, write-through progress, explicit chapter advancing.

Decisions baked into this design

  • Sidebar owns navigation + display settings (chapter tree, mode, fit, width, margins) and hides with one click / S. The top bar keeps only back · page indicator · toggle.
  • Custom margins apply in width-stretch modes (Strip and Pages-Fit-Width): slider 0–200px, keys , .; remembered together with mode and width per title.
  • Same tab, view swap; back/Esc restores the title page with its scroll.
  • Vault-only, full-size pages, next two prefetched; offline is the only mode.
  • Progress write-through: open → reading; last page / Continue → read.
  • Explicit chapter advancing: sidebar rows, [ ], end-of-chapter card. Never automatic.
  • Preview crop elsewhere: thumbnails (library strip, title pane) get an aspect cap for very tall pages; the reader itself never crops.
